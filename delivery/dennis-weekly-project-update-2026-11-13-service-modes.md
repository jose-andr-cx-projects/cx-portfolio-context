## Service and Interaction Modes mapping

**Decision:** Introduce a configurable, explainable mapping pass to facilitate human verification, not automate approval of Interaction Modes.

**Progress:** Evaluated all 198 distinct Service Offerings in the available working workbook (645 source rows). First pass produced 73 rule-supported Mode candidates and left 125 Open. These are hypotheses, not validated classifications.

**Value:** Makes review more efficient and consistent, records why each candidate was suggested, and allows criteria to be refined with evidence from human decisions.

**Strategic direction:** Validate customer-interaction grain with a varied sample, including multi-Mode offerings. Keep uncertain classifications visible and avoid chasing 100% coverage. Confirm catalogue freshness and Bitbucket source authority before operational adoption.

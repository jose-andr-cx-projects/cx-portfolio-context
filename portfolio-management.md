# Portfolio navigation and next-action review

**Status:** New programme architecture, not a verified live status report.

The portfolio links the nine delivery, governance, analytics and demonstration repositories and must not duplicate their project-level evidence or the official Jira backlog.

## Default programme review
For each relevant active project: intended outcome, verified evidence date, material change, next increment, cross-project dependency, decision needed, authoritative link. Report unknowns rather than made-up dates or owners.

## Cross-project opportunity lenses
- Business context connects customer/account research, service experience mapping and account analytics.
- Channel design may depend on service interaction modes and existing intake patterns.
- Communication governance and employee content governance may share methods, not necessarily owners or source content.
- Reusable CX knowledge is a governed capability exploration, not a substitute for source documents.

## Portfolio intake
User-provided latest project evidence triggers update proposals. No automated personal GitHub mirror. Future Strategic OS connection must be reviewed, selective and compliant with organisational information management requirements. See `strategic-os/future-intake.md`.

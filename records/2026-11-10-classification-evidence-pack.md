## 1. Request and basis

I request a formal review of my current position classification because the sustained scope, complexity, autonomy, specialist judgement and cross-functional influence of the work I undertake appear to have evolved beyond the existing position description. ## 2. Position-value hypothesis

**Proposition to test:** My role increasingly requires independent diagnosis of cross-service problems, development of reusable business context and CX capabilities, translation between operational, technical and design stakeholders, and influence on organisational decisions beyond discrete assigned project tasks.

**Not the claim:** Being busy, performing strongly, managing more projects or possessing leadership potential is alone sufficient for reclassification.

**Evidence standard:** Show (a) sustained duties, (b) independent judgement and delegated accountability, (c) decisions influenced, (d) demonstrable organisational reach, and (e) differences from the current signed PD. Separate *outputs created* from *outcomes adopted*.

## 4. Leadership: argument and fair counterargument

**Argument:** Leadership may be demonstrated through independent problem framing, influencing authorised decision-makers, cross-disciplinary alignment, capability-building and delivery coordination. In a hierarchical environment, lack of formal authority does not negate evidence of influence.

**Counterargument to address:** Class 6 may legitimately require a particular degree of authority, management skill or independent accountability. Leadership evidence must be assessed against the actual Appendix 11 descriptors, not assumed. Where an essential requirement is unmet, development cannot substitute for the requirement. Where a skill is an improvement opportunity rather than a prerequisite, it should not become an undefined barrier to reviewing work value.

**Ask of assessor:** Specify which descriptor is unmet, what evidence is missing, and whether the concern relates to current job requirements or future development.

# Channel Strategy Y2 — Convergence-to-Views Test

## Status

Active project test.

This is a project-specific experiment, not yet a reusable Strategic OS framework.

## Purpose

Test whether Channel Strategy Y2 can reduce duplicate synthesis work by creating one structured convergence of project evidence and then using that synthesis to generate multiple purpose-specific views.

The test is framed around the key shift:

> **Divergence creates evidence and possibilities. Convergence should create one synthesis, not multiple deliverables. Deliverables become views of that synthesis.**

## Problem being tested

Project teams often diverge successfully through research, workshops, analytics, experiments, prototypes and stakeholder input.

During convergence, however, the same evidence is repeatedly re-synthesised into separate artefacts such as:

- design briefs;
- workshop or Design Sprint inputs;
- presentations;
- Jira updates;
- recommendations;
- decision material; and
- executive value stories.

Each artefact partially reconstructs the same evidence and interpretation.

This creates:

- duplicated effort;
- inconsistent framing between artefacts;
- loss of evidence traceability;
- avoidable maintenance;
- pressure to prematurely simplify uncertainty; and
- additional work whenever the underlying understanding changes.

## Hypothesis

If Channel Strategy Y2 creates one explicit synthesis at convergence, then the Design Brief, Design Sprint inputs and Q1 executive value drop can be produced as different views of the same understanding rather than as separate synthesis exercises.

This should reduce production effort while improving consistency and traceability.

## Test context

Use the current Channel Strategy Y2 convergence as the first live test.

Evidence inputs may include:

- existing Discovery and Define learning;
- Intelligent Front Door Design Brief work;
- parking / infringement exploration;
- Off-Platform Search test learning;
- Sustainability POC learning; and
- relevant service, stakeholder or analytical evidence already available to the project.

Organisational systems remain authoritative for the original evidence and approved project material. This test should reference rather than duplicate controlled source material.

## Test model

### 1. Diverge

Allow evidence and possibilities to emerge through the methods appropriate to the work.

Do not force all divergent activity into a common schema while learning is still being generated.

Possible inputs include:

- research;
- observation;
- analytics;
- experiments;
- prototypes;
- service knowledge;
- stakeholder input; and
- operational learning.

### 2. Converge once

Bring relevant evidence into a single working synthesis.

Use the minimum useful structure:

| Source | Evidence / observation | Learning | IFD implication | Design requirement | Design question | Q1 value signal | Confidence / gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

The synthesis should preserve the distinction between evidence, interpretation, assumptions and open questions.

It is a working sensemaking layer, not a new organisational system of record.

### 3. Create views

Use the synthesis to generate the artefacts required for different purposes.

| View | Primary question |
| --- | --- |
| Intelligent Front Door Design Brief | What should we design for? |
| Design Sprint inputs | What remains important to explore or test? |
| Q1 executive value drop | What have we learned or made tangible, and why does it matter? |
| Jira | What work remains? |
| Decision material | What decision or direction requires leadership attention? |

Views may select, reorder or reframe synthesis content for their audience, but should not independently reconstruct the underlying evidence unless a genuine gap is found.

### 4. Diverge again

Design, prototyping, testing and stakeholder interaction will create new evidence.

Feed material new learning into the next convergence rather than continuously expanding previous deliverables.

## Channel Strategy test

### Test question

Can one convergence synthesis materially reduce the work required to complete the Intelligent Front Door Design Brief, prepare the Design Sprint and communicate the Q1 value story while maintaining or improving decision clarity?

### Test activities

1. Gather the minimum relevant evidence already produced through Q1.
2. Add learning from the Off-Platform Search test and Sustainability POC as it becomes available.
3. Complete the convergence synthesis once.
4. Use it to refine the Intelligent Front Door Design Brief.
5. Use the same synthesis to identify Design Sprint questions and scenarios.
6. Use the same synthesis to construct the Q1 executive narrative.
7. Record where a view required substantial new synthesis outside the common layer.
8. At the end, assess whether the approach reduced duplicate work and improved coherence.

## What to observe

The test should look for practical evidence of:

- less repeated synthesis;
- faster production of downstream artefacts;
- stronger consistency between Design and executive narratives;
- clearer evidence-to-interpretation traceability;
- easier identification of assumptions and gaps;
- easier incorporation of new learning;
- lower maintenance burden; and
- any cases where a single synthesis becomes too generic or constraining.

Do not create detailed measurement overhead. Qualitative evidence from real use is sufficient for this first test.

## Success signal

The pattern is useful if, by the end of the Q1 delivery:

- the synthesis materially contributes to at least the Design Brief, Design Sprint preparation and executive value drop;
- those views remain coherent without separately re-analysing the same evidence;
- José spends less effort reconciling duplicated project narratives; and
- the approach remains simple enough to use without becoming another maintained project artefact.

## Failure signal

Do not promote the pattern if:

- maintaining the synthesis creates more work than it saves;
- the views require substantially different evidence models;
- important nuance is lost by forcing evidence into the common structure;
- teams need to duplicate analysis anyway; or
- the synthesis becomes a new documentation layer with no clear decision or delivery value.

## End-of-test review

At the end of the Channel Strategy Y2 test, capture:

1. what worked;
2. what did not;
3. which views were successfully generated from the common synthesis;
4. where additional synthesis was still required;
5. whether the approach saved meaningful effort;
6. whether evidence traceability or decision clarity improved; and
7. whether the pattern is worth testing in another project.

## Candidate use case

If the test demonstrates useful reuse, create a concise use case showing:

> **How a project team can move from divergent evidence to one convergence synthesis and generate multiple delivery, Design and executive views without repeatedly re-synthesising the same material.**

The use case should be grounded in the actual Channel Strategy Y2 experience, including the artefacts produced, where effort was saved, limitations encountered and the conditions under which the pattern is useful.

Do not promote this into a reusable Strategic OS framework until real use demonstrates value beyond this project.
